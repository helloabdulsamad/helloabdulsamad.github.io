# Inkz ’N’ Paint: selling print online across Nigeria (client build)

**Status:** Built, not deployed yet
**Role:** Founder of Upsert Labs (the developer) and lead engineer · Oct 2026 – present · Client work; code is private

## 1. The problem
Inkz ’N’ Paint is a printing press that sells across Nigeria from one studio. It wanted its own web application to sell print nationwide, with prices customers can see for themselves, to compete with an established online printer. Customers want an instant price and print-ready artwork, even without a designer. Staff need one place to run orders, and customers far from the studio wait longer and pay more for delivery.

## 2. My role
I ran the project from first research to an agreed scope, including a full design and a clickable prototype. Then I built the web application and its print editor, and tested it end to end before deployment.

## 3. How it works
- Customers pick a product and its options and see the price straight away.
- They can supply artwork five ways: upload a file, use our online editor, design in Canva, hire a designer, or send it later.
- Staff run proofs, production, dispatch and prices from one place.
- Orders from other states can go to a vetted local partner printer, offered to the best-ranked partner first. It is built on WordPress and WooCommerce.

## 4. Key decisions
- **WooCommerce, not a custom shop.** The client's staff can run products, prices and delivery themselves after handover.
- **The server sets every price.** Customers can't tamper with prices, and every staff price change is logged.
- **Our own print editor instead of a monthly licence.** No recurring fee for the client, and it handles Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and Arabic text. Canva works through each customer's own account, which keeps within Canva's terms.
- **A fulfilment network, not a marketplace.** The press keeps the customer and the national price, and partners never see customers' contact details. Partner earnings are held for 72 hours after delivery, so complaints come first.

## 5. The outcome
- Built and tested; not deployed yet.
- In the pre-launch test, the main journeys worked, every price checked matched the price tables, and the partner flow ran from routing to delivery. One customer could not open another customer's orders.
- The first full build caught problems before launch. One would have left the test site open to search engines; another showed an old price after a staff edit. All were fixed.
