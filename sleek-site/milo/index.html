What Milo does

The questions you answer fifty times a day, handled.

Customers get a reply in seconds, even at midnight. You keep the conversations that need a human touch.

Finds the right product

Milo searches your live catalogue for price, colour, size and stock, so customers never hear about items you've sold out of.

Checks orders

"Where is my order?" gets a real answer. Each customer only ever sees their own orders.

Takes the order and the payment

Milo places the order and sends a Paystack payment link in the chat. No back and forth over account numbers.

Hands tricky cases to you

Complaints, special requests and anything Milo shouldn't answer come straight to you, with the conversation so far.

Built to be trusted with money

I built Milo for my own watch store, WatchShop.ng, so it had to be safe around real orders and real payments.

Abdulsamad Osunlana, founder, Upsert Labs

Payments confirmed once

The amount, currency and order are checked before a payment counts. No underpaid orders, and no order confirmed twice.

Customers see only their own orders

Milo knows who it's talking to from their verified WhatsApp number, never from what they type.

Never leaves a customer on blue ticks

If something goes wrong, the customer still gets a polite reply instead of silence.

No orders left hanging

If a payment link fails, the order is cancelled cleanly instead of left hanging.

Questions

Good to know

Is Milo live?

Milo is built for WatchShop.ng and is getting ready to go live. The version for other businesses is in progress. Join the waitlist and you'll hear first.

Do I need a developer to set it up?

No. The plan is a simple connect button for WhatsApp and an easy way to add your products.

Which payments does it support?

Milo sends Paystack payment links today. Other gateways can follow based on what waitlist businesses need.

Will it reply to customers I want to handle myself?

You stay in control. Milo hands complaints and special requests to you, and you can step into any chat.

How much will it cost?

Milo will be a simple monthly plan. Waitlist members hear about pricing first.
